# Review Checklist

## Scope Review

- Are all required first-level menus covered?
- Are all required second-level menus covered?
- Are all required third-level pages covered?
- Does the menu overview table reflect the actual menu tree?
- For H5, does the page overview reflect the real bottom-tab / homepage-entry / flow structure?
- Is the overview table specific enough to explain the business meaning of each page?

## Button Review

For each page:

- Are all major top buttons covered?
- Are list row actions covered?
- Are hidden or conditional buttons explained when relevant?
- Is each button outcome verified by an actual click?
- For workflow-trigger buttons like `去练习` or `创建`, is the next meaningful screen also covered?

## Screenshot Review

- Does each screenshot match the step above it?
- Are popup screenshots actual popup captures instead of base-page screenshots?
- Are button-triggered new pages captured separately?
- Are multi-step flows fully covered?
- For H5, are screenshots taken in mobile viewport rather than desktop viewport?
- Are downstream workflow screens captured where necessary?

## Numbering Review

- Are chapter numbers correct?
- Are figure numbers consistent?
- Are captions aligned with the page names?

## Accuracy Review

- Does the text match the current live UI?
- Are empty states described truthfully?
- Are disabled or unavailable functions explicitly marked?
- Are English route-style titles converted into Chinese product terminology when possible?
- Are context-dependent pages clearly marked as requiring upstream business entry?

## File Review

- Was only the user-specified target `.docx` modified?
- Does the `.docx` open normally?
- Are screenshots saved in the intended directory?
